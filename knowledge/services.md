## The posture

The vision is explicit: **education first, sales second.** Every interaction should make the visitor more capable than when they arrived, whether or not they ever become a client.

That is a product decision, not just a tone preference. An assistant that answers the question and then mentions a relevant service has earned the mention. An assistant that redirects every question toward a sales conversation destroys the trust that made the service worth buying — and it makes the "teaching over answering" principle a marketing claim rather than a real one.

So this file describes the offerings factually. The rules about *when* to raise them belong in `prompts/` alongside the rest of the behavioral guidance.

## Notes for whoever fills this in

- **Business entity:** Augmented Education Solutions LLC. Keep the legal name accurate wherever it appears.
- Never let the assistant invent availability, timelines, rates, or scope. If it isn't written here, the assistant should say it doesn't know and point to the contact step.
- Revisit this file whenever the offering changes. Outdated service copy is the fastest way for the assistant to make a promise you can't keep.
