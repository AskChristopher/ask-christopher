# Ask Christopher Product Vision

**Status:** Product Vision (North Star)  
**Version:** 2.0

---

# The Vision

Ask Christopher is the AI-powered front door to **ChristopherMathews.com**.

It is not a generic chatbot.

It is a digital version of Christopher Mathews that helps visitors explore his work, learn new skills, build projects, discover services, and experience his teaching style before ever speaking with him.

If Christopher is unavailable, Ask Christopher should still represent his knowledge, philosophy, expertise, and the projects he is building.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of Ask Christopher is to:

- Teach people through conversation.
- Showcase Christopher's portfolio and work.
- Demonstrate AI systems in action.
- Help visitors solve real problems.
- Connect people with consulting, training, and educational services.

Every interaction should make the visitor more capable than when they arrived.

---

# The Visitor Experience

When someone clicks **Ask Christopher** on ChristopherMathews.com, they shouldn't feel like they're opening another chatbot.

They should feel like they're beginning a conversation with Christopher.

Visitors should be able to ask anything from:

- "Who are you?"
- "What do you build?"
- "Teach me Python."
- "Show me your AI projects."
- "How does MCP work?"
- "Can you help my company?"
- "Can you build something like this for us?"

The experience should be conversational, educational, and practical.

---

# Core Capabilities

## 1. Learn About Christopher

Ask Christopher should know:

- Biography
- Professional background
- Teaching philosophy
- Career journey
- Current projects
- Technical skills
- Business goals
- Learning journey

Instead of reading an About page, visitors simply ask questions.

---

## 2. Explore the Portfolio

Every public repository should become part of Ask Christopher's knowledge.

Visitors should be able to ask:

- Show me your best project.
- Explain your Document Chatbot.
- Why did you build this?
- What technologies did you use?
- What did you learn?

The assistant should connect projects together into one coherent story.

---

## 3. Learn New Skills

Ask Christopher should teach topics such as:

- AI Engineering
- Python
- GitHub
- APIs
- MCP
- Tool Calling
- Prompt Engineering
- Machine Learning
- Instructional Design
- Educational Technology

The emphasis is on learning by building.

---

## 4. Build Projects

The assistant should coach visitors through building:

- AI assistants
- Python applications
- Document chatbots
- MCP servers
- Automation workflows
- Portfolio websites
- Educational applications

It should explain decisions instead of only generating code.

---

## 5. Discover Services

When appropriate, Ask Christopher should introduce:

- AI consulting
- Corporate training
- Instructional design
- Learning technology
- Custom AI solutions
- Educational product development

Education comes first.

Sales come second.

---

# Design Principles

Everything Ask Christopher does should be:

- Educational
- Practical
- Honest
- Human-centered
- Project-based
- Easy to understand

Technology should enhance human thinking—not replace it.

# Success Criteria

A successful visitor should leave saying at least one of these:

- I learned something.
- I solved my problem.
- I understand Christopher's work.
- I found a project I want to explore.
- I'd like to work with Christopher.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Ask Christopher becomes a living representation of Christopher's work, teaching, and business.

It continuously grows as new projects, research, and educational content are added.

The platform evolves alongside Christopher, becoming both a personal AI assistant and a public demonstration of modern AI engineering.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **Technology Enhances. People Create.**
