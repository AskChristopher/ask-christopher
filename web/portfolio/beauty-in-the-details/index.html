01 The problem

A word most people have never heard

Coloboma is a gap in part of the eye, present from birth. When it affects the iris, it can give the pupil a keyhole shape. Most people have never heard the word.

The goal was to give a general audience a short, accurate, kind explanation they could watch in the time it takes to scroll past it.

02 The constraints

Ninety seconds, no prior knowledge

  • Length: 75 to 90 seconds, short enough to watch all the way through.
  • Audience: anyone. No medical vocabulary beyond the one word being taught.
  • Look: the video had to share the visual language of the Beauty in the Details shirt, so the two read as one message.
03 The build

From a shirt to a script to code

  1. 01Design

    Original T-shirt artwork sets the palette and style.

  2. 02Script

    Written for a listener with no medical background.

  3. 03Animate

    Scenes built in Remotion, video written as code.

  4. 04Voice

    Narration and music produced in ElevenLabs.

  5. 05Publish

    Released on YouTube with a call to action.

Why Remotion. Remotion builds video from code (React components rendered frame by frame). Every scene is a reusable, editable component, so changing the timing, a word, or a color means editing a value and re-rendering, not rebuilding a timeline by hand.

Why my own voice. The narration is an AI clone of my own voice, made with ElevenLabs, and the background music was generated there too. I wanted the speed of generated audio without handing the message to a stranger's voice.

04 The result

Published, and built to be updated

The finished video is published on YouTube and embedded above. It shares its visual world with the Beauty in the Details shirt, so the shirt and the video carry one message.

It shows a different side of my work from the other pieces here: short-form media for the public rather than training for employees, where the whole learning experience has to fit in about a minute and a half.
