## Context

What situation forced a decision? What constraints were in play — technical, educational, budgetary, time? Write enough that someone unfamiliar with the moment can understand the pressure without asking you.

State facts, not conclusions. If a benchmark, cost figure, or vision-document requirement drove the decision, cite it here.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated plainly and in the active voice.

> We will …

## Alternatives considered

For each realistic option that was not chosen:

**Option name** — what it is, what was attractive about it, and the specific reason it lost. "It was worse" is not a reason; "it adds a vector database and an embedding pipeline to solve a problem we don't have at this corpus size" is.

The honest inclusion of a rejected option's *strengths* is what makes this section worth reading later.

## Consequences

What becomes easier as a result. What becomes harder. What is now expensive to undo.

Include the costs you accepted knowingly — those are the ones a future reader will otherwise assume you overlooked.

## Revisit when

The concrete trigger that should reopen this decision. A threshold, an event, or a date — not "if it becomes a problem."
